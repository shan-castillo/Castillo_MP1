Google Maps review by Tito Ramos


The Calasiao puto here is really delicious, soft, fresh, and available in many flavors. They also have a wide variety of pasalubong and local delicacies. Since I discovered this place, I always buy my Calasiao puto here. I keep coming back because the puto is really good and there are many choices. The only downside is the limited parking, especially when it gets busy. There are also quite a few people asking for money around the area, which can sometimes make the experience uncomfortable. Overall, this is still a good place to stop if you are looking for delicious Calasiao puto and local pasalubong.
